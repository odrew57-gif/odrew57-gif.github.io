To solve this problem, I thought back to my own experiences as a furniture retailer, and blended that with research-based observations about the customer journey. In the end, it drove revenue up for our clients, and enabled us to go deploy best practices at scale across our platform.

Brand StrategySelling Process
SaaS Marketing Strategy — 10x Warm Lead Growth

At the time of this project's start, the company struggled to capture leads from any marketing channels besides word-of-mouth referral. We were spending too much money on advertising channels that didn't attribute qualified leads. So, I took one of the company's original marketing gimmicks from its inception, and turned it into a functioning powerhouse to drive leads. We built the "website assessment", a simple, no-pressure offer to get an outside perspective on what you did well online, and what you didn't. We deployed this messaging into our existing paid channels, and immediately saw an increase in leads. We went from nurturing 4 or 5 "iffy at best" leads a month to 50 leads with tangible actions to take next steps.

Marketing Agency Partner Program

We faced a serious problem: marketing agencies found us difficult to work with. As a result, we spent lots of unnecessary hours creating landing pages for agencies who didn't want to collaborate with us. To fix this, I created the Marketing Agency Partner Program. This program allowed us to approach advertisers and marketers with a free-to-use, easy solution to create more expansive campaigns for our mutual clients, and increase their billable hours. This built better trust between agencies and our company, and created a better campaign output for dealers already using our platform. Not only that, we were able to significantly reduce account managers' time spent building ad pages that served minimal results.

Paid SearchSEO & GEO
Paid Search Pivot — 300% User Growth, 15× ROAS

A small, family owned client who had been around since the 50's was struggling to see the same ROI on their traditional advertising they were so used to. When I learned about this problem, I did an analysis of their market, their existing traffic, and existing campaigns to realize that they were not invested into Digital Paid Channels. I built a from-scratch Paid Search campaign that targeted a very specific, but highly valuable chunk of their customer-base. After the first few months of brand awareness and exposure in Paid Search, we started to see the results flow. Customers who had shopped with them for years finally understood that they were an E-Commerce capable company, and it drove a 15x ROI on the ad spend just from the Paid Search channel itself. Total attributed return could be categorized as well over 50x.
